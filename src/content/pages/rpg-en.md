This is where I want to bring the adventures from the tabletop campaign I've been
running and give them a place of their own. The campaign is called
**Children of the Storm**. It began in 2025 and is still underway, with the finale
expected around 2028.

Over the past two years, I've been writing the adventure, its characters, and the
threads that connect them. Much of it lived in notes in my Notion. This section is
my way of giving it all a proper home.

We play **Tormenta 20**, a system published by **Jambô Editora**, a Brazilian game
publisher. I also have a long-standing soft spot for **Vampire: The Masquerade**,
and some of what I share here will draw on that fondness for darker stories
centered on their characters.

## What you'll find here

- **The world.** Places, factions, and the lore behind Children of the Storm.
- **Sessions.** Recaps and the loose threads still in play.
- **Behind the screen.** How I build encounters, pace an arc, and keep a long campaign coherent.

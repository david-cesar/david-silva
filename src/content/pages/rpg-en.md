This is where I bring the tabletop side of my work into a place that can hold it.
I run a campaign called **Filhos da Tormenta**, started in 2025 and still going,
with an ending planned for around 2028. For two years now I have been writing its
world, its characters, and the threads that tie them together, and most of that
has lived in scattered notes. This section is the effort to give it a proper home.

We play **Tormenta 20**, the system published by **Jambô Editora**, a Brazilian
studio. I also have a long-standing soft spot for **Vampire: The Masquerade**, and
some of what shows up here will lean on that love for a darker, character-first
kind of story.

I keep saying, on this site and off it, that the work I enjoy is turning
complexity into something another person can pick up and use. A campaign is
exactly that problem in another form, so expect the same instinct here: notes made
legible, decisions explained, a world you could step into.

## What is coming here

- **The world.** Places, factions, and the lore behind Filhos da Tormenta.
- **Sessions.** Recaps and the loose threads still in play.
- **Behind the screen.** How I build encounters, pace an arc, and keep a long campaign coherent.

Nothing published yet. The first entries are on their way.
